Purpose
To support exemplary minority students attending a two or four year college or university upon graduation from Kankakee High School.
Award
Up to $2,500 in scholarship awards, presented by the Amos & Edith Wallace Scholarship Selection Committee.
Eligibility
  • Kankakee High School Graduating Senior
  • Minority Student
  • Attending a 2 or 4 year college or university, Fall 2026
  • Minimum 2.8 GPA
  • Strong Character & Integrity
Required to Apply
  • Completed application form
  • Most recent high school transcript
  • Proof of college acceptance (when available)
  • 2 Letters of Recommendation — Teacher/Counselor + Community Leader
  • Personal Essay (400–600 words, separate document)

Personal Essay

Respond to all three prompts in a single 400–600 word document and attach it to your submission email.

  1. Besides academics, what life lessons are you most interested to learn throughout your college experience, and why?
  2. What role has community service played in your life, and how will you incorporate service into your collegiate career?
  3. What role has education played in your life, and what academic goals will you set for your first year? How will you achieve them?

Submit your essay as a separate Word or PDF document attached to your application email.
